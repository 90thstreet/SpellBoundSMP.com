What is Spellbound SMP?
SpellBound is a custom Minecraft SMP built around a powerful magic-based plugin. With unique spell combinations, legendary weapons & armour, and rare one-of-a-kind abilities, no two players fight the same way. This isn’t just a server, it’s a growing community driven by magic.
Our Goal
SpellBound isn’t just about power, it’s about people. Our goal is to create one of the most welcoming and active SMP communities possible. We believe every player matters and we are building a magical world where everyone has a place.
The Spell System
⚔️ Spell System
Players can equip 3 spells at once:
  • 1 Offensive
  • 1 Defensive
  • 1 Utility
🌌 Spell Themes
  • Divinity
  • Arcana
  • Elemental
Using 3 spells from the same theme grants powerful bonus effects.
📜 18 Unique Spells
  • Craftable and accessible to all players
  • Each spell has its own ability, cost, and theme
  • Endless combinations and playstyles
💠 Mana System
  • Mana bar displayed on-screen
  • Regenerates over time
  • Required to cast spells strategically
