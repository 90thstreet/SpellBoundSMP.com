What is Spellbound SMP?
SpellBound is a custom Minecraft SMP built around a powerful magic-based plugin. With unique spell combinations, legendary weapons & armour, and rare abilities, no two players fight the same way.
Our Goal
Our goal is to create one of the most welcoming and active SMP communities possible. We’re building more than a server, we’re building a magical world where everyone has a place.
The Spell System
⚔️ Spell System
Players can equip 3 spells at once:
  • 1 Offensive
  • 1 Defensive
  • 1 Utility
🌌 Spell Themes
  • Divinity
  • Arcana
  • Elemental
📜 18 Unique Spells
  • Craftable and accessible to all players
  • Each spell has unique abilities and costs
  • Endless combinations and playstyles
💠 Mana System
  • Mana bar displayed on-screen
  • Regenerates over time
  • Required to cast spells strategically
